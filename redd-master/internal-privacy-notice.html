Data Handling Notice

Redd Master 1.0 — internal notice for staff and IT. Not a public store policy.

Effective date: __________  ·  Last updated: __________

Summary Redd Master does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device until you choose to export it yourself.

1. Scope

This notice describes how the Redd Master application handles data. It is provided for staff and for the IT department. Application owner: __________________________. Contact: __________________________.

2. Information the app collects

We collect nothing. The app has no server component. No information is transmitted to us or to any third party at any time, including when the device has a network connection.

The app stores the following on your device only:

Stored on the deviceWhy
Survey records you enter — counts, measurements, sample numbers, notes This is the purpose of the app
A name and initials you type in to identify who is recording So field records can be attributed to a person for data-quality follow-up
Photographs you choose to take within the app To document specimens and locations
Location coordinates, only when you press the GPS button or enable auto-capture To record where an observation was made
Your display settingsTo remember your preferences

3. Location

The app requests location access only when you ask it to record a position. Coordinates are written into your survey record on the device. They are never sent anywhere. You may decline location access entirely and enter coordinates by hand; all other features continue to work.

4. Camera and photographs

The app requests camera access only when you choose to attach a photograph to a record. Photographs are stored on the device alongside your survey data and are never uploaded.

5. How your data leaves the device

Only when you deliberately export it. Exporting writes a file (Excel, CSV, JSON, SQL, or a ZIP of photographs) to your device, which you may then send using whatever method your organisation uses. The app performs no automatic upload and no background transmission.

Once you send an exported file, its handling is governed by your organisation's own data policies, not by this policy.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information, because we do not receive any. There are no advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting services or third-party software development kits in the app.

7. Children

The app is a professional field tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no information at all, it collects none from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records or an entire survey inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data. Because we never receive your data, we cannot retrieve, restore or delete it on your behalf.

9. Security

Data is stored in the app's own storage on the device and is protected by your device's security — passcode, biometrics and encryption. Since nothing is transmitted, there is no transmission to intercept.

Please note The name shown as "Recorded by" identifies who entered a record. It is not a login and does not verify identity or restrict access to the device.

10. Your rights

Applicable privacy laws give you rights to access, correct and delete your personal information. Because all of your information is held on your own device and never sent to us, you exercise these rights directly: view and edit records in the app, delete them, or uninstall the app.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
